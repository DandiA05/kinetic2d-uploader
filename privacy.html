Privacy Policy

Kinetic2D Uploader is a personal application designed to upload videos to YouTube using the YouTube Data API.

Information Collection

This application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the user's YouTube account with authorized permissions.

Data Usage

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally and used only to perform authorized YouTube upload operations.

Data Sharing

This application does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through their Google account.
